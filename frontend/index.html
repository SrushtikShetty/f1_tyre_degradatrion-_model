Explainability

This benchmark predicts a simulated next-lap tyre-wear increment using features available at the stated prediction moment. Saved importance values describe predictive association only; they do not establish physical causality or a local effect.

The feature policy excludes target and future-state fields. No SHAP or local feature-attribution method is currently computed.
